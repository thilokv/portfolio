# Staff User Guide

CareOS works on your Android phone browser (Chrome) and on computers. Use your **own** account — never share passwords.

## Signing in
1. Open the CareOS address, enter your e-mail and password.
2. First time: you must set a new password (10+ characters with letters and numbers).
3. You are signed out automatically after 60 minutes of inactivity. Lock your phone when not in use.
4. Switch language any time with **EN / తెలుగు** at the top or bottom of the menu.

## Your dashboard
Shows today's appointments, completed sessions, session notes still pending, reviews due in the next 30 days, staff availability and recent activity. All numbers come from the records in the system.

## Children
- **Children** lists the children on your care team (coordinators and administrators see all). Search by name or ID (e.g. `CDC-0004`), filter by status or service.
- A child's record has tabs: Overview, Guardians, Development history, Assessments, Development plan, Sessions, Progress, Documents, Communication and (for coordinators/admins) Record history. You only see tabs your role allows.

## Assessments
1. Child → **Assessments → New assessment**.
2. Choose your professional role and service, date, the **exact tool name and version** you used.
3. Record findings and observations only for the developmental areas you assessed; leave others empty.
4. **Save** keeps a draft you can edit. **Finalize** locks it permanently.
- CareOS never diagnoses or calculates standardized scores. Record only what you observed and your documented professional conclusions.
- Psychological assessments are automatically restricted.

## Development plan (IDP)
1. **New plan** with start and review dates and family priorities. A coordinator activates it.
2. **Add goal**: area, baseline (with how/when measured), a measurable target (behaviour + criterion + conditions), measurement method, strategies, responsible professional, review date, status.
3. **Revise goal** creates a new version; the old one is kept in history with your reason.
4. **Record review** captures the review date, summary and new status.

## Scheduling and sessions
1. **Schedule → New appointment**: child, therapist (from the child's care team), service, start time, duration, optional weekly repeat. Double bookings are blocked.
2. On the day: **Check in** when the child arrives (late arrivals are flagged automatically).
3. **Start session** (only the assigned therapist) opens the session note.
4. Fill in attendance, activities, child response, your service-specific details (none are mandatory), goals addressed and, if you measured them, total/successful/independent trials and assistance level. **Leave trial boxes empty if you did not measure — empty is never counted as zero.**
5. **Save note** as often as you like. **Complete session** when finished, then **Sign note**. Signed notes are locked and cannot be changed.
6. **Cancel** or **No-show** for appointments that did not happen.

## Progress
Shows each goal's baseline, target, a chart of recorded success rates and the observation log. Trend statements appear only with at least 3 measured observations and are worded cautiously. Use **Record observation** for measurements taken outside a session. **Create report draft** builds a factual draft (attendance and counts only) for you to complete.

## Families
- **Communication** tab → **New message to family** for updates or home activity suggestions. If you add Telugu text, you must tick "I have reviewed the Telugu text". Do not translate diagnoses or assessment conclusions without professional review.
- Reports: write → **Submit for approval** → a coordinator (or another approver) **Approves** → **Share with family**. Families see only shared reports.
- Messages from families appear in blue; click **Mark as read** once handled.

## Documents
Upload PDF/JPG/PNG/WEBP (max 10 MB). Tick "Restricted" for psychological/sensitive files and "Visible to family" only for documents meant for parents.

## AI assistant (optional)
Helps draft activity ideas, note structure, summaries and parent wording. It never diagnoses or prescribes. Never type names or phone numbers (they are removed automatically anyway). Everything it produces is a **draft** — check every sentence against your own observations, then copy what you need into the record yourself.

## Good practice
- Write facts first ("Named 3 of 5 pictures with a verbal prompt"), label interpretations.
- Complete and sign notes the same day.
- Report anything unusual (wrong access, lost phone) to the administrator immediately.
